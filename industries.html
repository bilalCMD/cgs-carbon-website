Activated Carbon Applications

Columbiana Grinding Services provides activated carbon processing for various industries.

It is worth being precise about the relationship. Our customers are the companies who market activated carbon; the industries below are the markets those companies supply. We work a step behind the end user, on the processing that makes the material fit for the market it is entering.

That is why a single facility can serve a wide range of industries without claiming expertise in each of them. The material and its preparation are what we know; the application knowledge sits with the company marketing it.

Municipal water & wastewater

Waterworks and treatment plants use activated carbon for taste and odour control, dissolved organics and effluent polishing.

Water applications

Manufacturing

Chemical & industrial

Background

What activated lignite is.

General information about the material family. The notes below describe activated carbon and lignite-based carbons in general terms rather than making claims about any particular branded product.

01

Lignite as a raw material

Lignite, sometimes called brown coal, is one of several raw materials from which activated carbons are produced. Others in common industrial use include hard coal, coconut shell and wood.

02

Activation develops porosity

Activation is the thermal treatment that opens up an internal pore structure in the carbon. The result is a material with a very large internal surface area in proportion to its mass.

03

Adsorption on that surface

Substances in a passing liquid or gas stream adhere to the internal surface of the carbon. This is adsorption — accumulation on a surface — as distinct from absorption into the bulk of a material.

04

Powder and granular forms

Activated carbons are supplied in a range of physical forms. Powdered grades are typically dosed into a stream; granular grades are more often retained in a bed through which the stream passes.

05

Form follows the duty

Which form suits a given application depends on how the carbon will be introduced, retained and separated, and on the equipment already installed at the site using it.

06

Where processing comes in

Bringing a carbon into the physical form a market requires is a processing step. That step is the work of Columbiana Grinding Services.

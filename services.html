What we do

Processing Carbon.

Our standard processing covers pulverizing, screening and blending.

  • Pulverizing — fine grinding down to 95% passing 325 mesh
  • Screening — granular cuts such as 4x10 and 8x30 mesh, other sizes on request
  • Blending — blending of various products

Custom processing is also a capability. Should you require alternative processing techniques, we will work together with you to develop solutions for your needs.

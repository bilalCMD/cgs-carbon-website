Our specialization

Grinding carbon.

The company has spent years perfecting the art of grinding carbon. The notes below describe what grinding does to a carbon material in general terms.

  1. Size reduction

    Grinding reduces a carbon from the form it was manufactured in to a smaller, more uniform particle size. What leaves the mill is determined by the material going in and by how the mill is set up to treat it.

  2. Access to the surface

    Activated carbon works on its internal surface. Reducing particle size shortens the path to that surface, which is why finer grades are chosen where uptake needs to be quick.

  3. Fit for the duty

    Finer is not automatically better. Coarser material is easier to retain and handle in some duties, and gives a lower pressure drop in others. The right result is the one that suits the application the carbon is going into.

  4. Consistency between runs

    For a company marketing carbon, what matters is not one good batch but the same result next time. Repeatability is the reason grinding is described as an art perfected over years rather than a single operation.

The description of grinding above is general information about how size reduction affects carbon materials. Specific equipment, tolerances and particle-size ranges are not published here until confirmed.

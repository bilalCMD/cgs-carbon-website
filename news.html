Insight · Product basics

What activated lignite is — and what it isn't

Activated lignite sits in the same family as conventional activated carbon but takes a different route to the same job: continuous activation of freshly mined lignite rather than batch processing of purchased feedstock. That difference shows up in consistency and cost, which is why the material has held its place in high-consumption duties like leachate treatment and flue gas cleaning for decades.

Insight · Grade selection

Powder or granulate? Start with your separation step

The fastest way to shortlist a grade is to look downstream, not upstream: if your plant already separates sludge well, powder dosing into the biological stage is a light retrofit; if you have vessels and headroom for a packed bed, granulate gives predictable service intervals. Contact time, pressure drop, and consumption economics settle the rest.

Insight · Trials

Before you trial a sorbent, settle these three questions

Most disappointing sorbent trials were designed to disappoint: no baseline data on the untreated stream, no agreed success metric, and no plan for how the loaded carbon leaves the process. Settle those three before the first bag arrives and the trial will tell you something either way — which is the point.

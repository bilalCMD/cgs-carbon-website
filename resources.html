What does CGS Carbon do?

CGS Carbon, Inc. provides services to those marketing activated carbon into various industries. Established in 2008, the company has focused on the processing and grinding of carbon.

What is your specialty?

CGS Carbon specializes in HOK® Activated Lignite.

How long have you been established?

Since 2008. The company has spent years perfecting the art of grinding carbon.

Where are you located?

41738 Esterly Drive, Columbiana, Ohio 44408 — close to the Ohio-Pennsylvania state line, approximately 20 miles south of Youngstown.

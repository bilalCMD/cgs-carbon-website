Our history

Established in 2008.

Established in 2008, CGS Carbon has spent years perfecting the art of grinding carbon.

Our experience covers coconut, bituminous, wood and lignite-based carbons, both virgin and regenerated. We specialize in HOK® Activated Lignite.

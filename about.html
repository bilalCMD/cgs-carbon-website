Our history

About CGS Carbon, Inc.

CGS Carbon, Inc. (formerly Columbiana Grinding Services) has spent nearly two decades perfecting the art of processing activated carbon.

Founded in 2008 and headquartered in Columbiana, Ohio, we specialize in processing activated carbon and are a domestic supplier of HOK® Activated Lignite, supporting partners who market activated carbon across a range of industries.

Our processing capabilities serve critical environmental and industrial applications, including water and wastewater treatment, hazardous waste incineration, mercury removal from flue gas, and soil decontamination. Whether the end use is municipal water treatment or industrial emissions control, we deliver consistently ground, high-quality activated carbon that our partners and their customers can depend on.

CGS Carbon is also expanding its capabilities to include carbon reactivation services, with new capacity planned for 2027 — reinforcing our commitment to closing the loop for our partners’ activated carbon needs.

As CGS Carbon, we carry forward the craftsmanship and reliability that built our reputation, while looking ahead to the next generation of carbon processing solutions.

Who we are

A privately held processing company.

Our customers are not the end users of activated carbon. They are the companies who market it.

Columbiana Grinding Services, LLC is a privately held organization providing services to those marketing activated carbon into various industries. That places the company at a particular point in the supply chain: after the carbon has been manufactured, and before it reaches the industry that will use it.

It is a position that suits a company built around a single activity. Rather than holding a broad catalogue or competing with the businesses we serve, we concentrate on the processing work those businesses need done — and we have concentrated on it since 2008.

  • Privately held — a privately held organization
  • Serving the market — providing services to those marketing activated carbon into various industries
  • One focus — the processing and grinding of carbon

Our history

Established in 2008.

Established in 2008, Columbiana Grinding Services has spent years perfecting the art of grinding carbon.

The phrase the company uses about itself is a careful one: it has spent years perfecting the art of grinding carbon. Grinding a material to a consistent, repeatable result is not a matter of switching on a machine. It is an accumulation of judgement about how a particular material behaves, and that judgement is built up over time rather than bought.

Since 2008 that accumulation has been the company's principal asset. Every year of operation has been spent on the same narrow question, applied to the same family of materials, at the same facility in Columbiana, Ohio.

The result is a company that can be described in a single line — and that regards the brevity of the description as the point.

  1. A single activity

    Processing and grinding of carbon, rather than a broad catalogue of unrelated services.

  2. A single specialty

    HOK® Activated Lignite is named as the specialty of the company.

  3. A single location

    One processing facility, in Columbiana, Ohio.

  4. A single kind of customer

    Those marketing activated carbon into various industries.

Our specialty

HOK® Activated Lignite.

Columbiana Grinding Services specializes in HOK® Activated Lignite.

Of everything the company handles, this is the product it names. A specialty of this kind is worth more than a general claim of competence: it says that the material is familiar, that its behaviour under processing is understood, and that the questions a customer is likely to ask have been answered before.

Who we are

Focused by design.

Most carbon suppliers carry a catalog. We carry a product.

CGS Carbon was formed to bring a proven European sorbent to the North American market with the depth a single product deserves: knowing its grades, how it behaves in biological and adsorptive service, what the manufacturer's documentation does and doesn't support, and how to move industrial quantities across the Atlantic reliably.

That focus is deliberate. When your treatment line depends on a sorbent, you want a supplier who has answered your question before — not one paging through a datasheet alongside you.

  • One product line — HOK® Activated Lignite, in all four grades
  • One market — North American water and gas treatment operators
  • One standard — claims backed by the manufacturer's documentation

Our parent company

Part of Birchtech.

CGS Carbon is a subsidiary of Birchtech, an environmental technology company whose business is clean air and clean water.

Birchtech develops sorbent and ion-exchange technologies for environmental duty — from mercury emissions capture at power utilities to PFAS treatment in water. Sorbents are not a sideline for the group; they are the business. CGS Carbon extends that same footing into activated lignite supply.

For customers, the parent matters in practical ways: financial substance behind supply commitments, group experience in sorbent duty on both the air and water side, and a long-term view of the North American treatment market.

Manufacturing & sourcing

Made by RWE in Germany.

We supply the product; we are careful to say we do not make it.

HOK® Activated Lignite is manufactured by RWE in Germany, produced continuously from freshly mined Rhenish lignite in a rotary hearth furnace at the source. Production runs under an ISO 9001-certified quality system, with material sampled multiple times daily and analyzed in the manufacturer's own laboratory.

That separation of roles is a feature, not a caveat: manufacturing stays with an industrial-scale producer with decades of process history, and supply, logistics, and application support for North America sit with a dedicated specialist.

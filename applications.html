Background

One material, many duties.

Activated carbon is used across a wide range of industries because a single property — a very large internal surface area on which substances adhere — turns out to be useful in a great many settings. What changes between one application and the next is the stream being treated, the substance being removed, and the physical form the carbon needs to be in.

The sections below describe activated carbon applications in general. They are not a statement of the specific industries or applications served by Columbiana Grinding Services; those are listed under to be confirmed until the company confirms them.

Application 01

Water treatment.

The largest and most familiar family of activated carbon applications.

In drinking water treatment, carbon is used to reduce taste and odour compounds and dissolved organic matter ahead of disinfection. In wastewater and industrial effluent, it is applied to organics that resist biological treatment. In both cases the carbon may be dosed into the stream as a powder and removed with the solids, or held in a bed through which the water passes.

  • Municipal drinking water — taste, odour and dissolved organics
  • Wastewater and effluent — organics that resist biological treatment
  • Point-of-use filtration — domestic and commercial filter media

Application 02

Air & gas purification.

The same adsorption principle, applied to a gas stream rather than a liquid one.

Vapour-phase applications range from odour control on industrial and municipal sites, through solvent recovery in manufacturing, to the treatment of flue and waste gas streams where operators must meet emissions limits. Carbon may be injected into the stream as a fine powder or installed as a packed bed depending on the duty.

  • Odour control — industrial and municipal air streams
  • Solvent recovery — capturing volatile organic compounds
  • Flue and waste gas — treatment ahead of discharge

Application 03

Process & product purification.

Where the carbon is used to clean up a product rather than a waste stream.

Across food, beverage, chemical and pharmaceutical manufacturing, activated carbon is used to decolourise liquids, remove trace impurities and refine a product to a required standard. These duties often call for finer material, because the carbon must act quickly and then be separated cleanly from the product.

  • Decolourising — sugar, syrups and other process liquids
  • Beverage production — refining and purification steps
  • Chemical and pharmaceutical — removal of trace impurities

Application 04

Environmental & industrial.

The demanding end of the range, where streams are variable and consumption is high.

Groundwater remediation, landfill leachate and industrial process streams present contaminant loads that change over time and are rarely uniform. Where consumption of carbon is high, the cost and consistency of the material become as important as its performance — which is one reason the processing step behind it matters.

  • Groundwater remediation — contaminated site treatment
  • Landfill leachate — variable, demanding streams
  • Industrial process streams — site-specific duties

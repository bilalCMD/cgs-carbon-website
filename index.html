The company

Focused on Processing Activated Carbon.

Established in 2008, CGS Carbon has spent years perfecting the art of grinding carbon.

Our experience spans coconut, bituminous, wood and lignite-based carbons, both virgin and reactivated. We specialize in virgin-based activated lignite, commonly known as HOK®.
